[Official rate-limits documentation](https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits). | **DOCUMENTED public default.** Whether Kairema has Free plan, which org owns the configured key, the live limits/remaining/reset and whether two model rows add to an independent aggregate are **NOT AVAILABLE / UNKNOWN**. [Official rate-limits documentation](https://docs.orcarouter.ai/operations/rate-limits). | **DOCUMENTED.** Current workspace plan, bucket size, remaining quota, reset, billing and model allocation are **NOT AVAILABLE / UNKNOWN**.
