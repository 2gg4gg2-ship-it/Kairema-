THIRD-PARTY NOTICES
==================

This file records the third-party components redistributed inside the Kairema Android APK by the
on-device terminal, where each one came from, and the licence each upstream project states.

SCOPE AND LIMITATION
--------------------
The texts below are provenance and licence identification, reproduced or referenced from the upstream
projects. They are not a legal review and not a legal opinion. Whether these notices are sufficient
for a particular distribution of the APK is a decision for whoever owns that distribution. No licence
text is paraphrased or invented here; each entry points at the authoritative source, and where the
full text is bundled in this repository it is reproduced verbatim from the package.

The debs this project was built from are not redistributed in this repository; only the extracted
binaries under android/app/src/main/ are. The upstream packages and their licence files remain
available at the URLs given.

VERIFICATION
------------
Every SHA-256 above is the real digest of the file that ships. The three debs were checked against the
SHA-256 values published in the Termux package index, and the rootfs was checked against the
.sha256 file published by Alpine. scripts/verify-terminal-apk.mjs recomputes the digests of the
extracted binaries and the rootfs directly from the built APK and fails the build on any mismatch.

Reproduce with:

  unzip -p android/app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk \
    assets/terminal/native/arm64-v8a/proot | sha256sum
  node scripts/verify-terminal-apk.mjs
